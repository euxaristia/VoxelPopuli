# Overworld creatures

The roster now contains 77 creature types, up from eight. Models and pixel
textures are original procedural assets, with separate head, limb, wing, tail,
and tentacle joints. Walking follows actual distance traveled; a blocked mob
stops its stride. Models face the same direction as their movement.

## In the world

Natural encounters use the terrain already generated by VoxelPopuli:

- Plains: pigs, cows, sheep, chickens, horses, donkeys, rabbits, and bees.
- Snowy taiga and tundra: wolves, foxes, polar bears, and strays, alongside
  the existing animal population where applicable.
- Mountains and high hills: goats and llamas.
- Desert: camels and camel husks; husks and parched after dark.
- Water: cod, salmon, tropical fish, pufferfish, dolphins, squid, nautiluses,
  zombie nautiluses, turtles, and drowned. Rivers and oceans currently share
  the water encounter pool because the generator does not distinguish them.
- Caves: bats, slimes, underground glow squid, and ordinary hostiles.
- Night: zombies, zombie villagers, skeletons, creepers, spiders, endermen,
  and zombie horses. Night encounters also appear near the player after
  existing chunks have loaded.
- Villages: villagers, iron golems, and cats.

Spawning checks loaded terrain, body clearance, water depth, occupied space,
and the shared 48-creature limit. Swimming mobs stay in water and flying mobs
maintain altitude. Neutral creatures retaliate after being hit; passive land
animals flee. Skeleton variants and pillagers shoot arrows. Melee and ranged
attacks require a clear line of sight. Cows, sheep, mooshrooms, and goats accept
wheat using the existing feeding and breeding system.

## Sandbox catalogue

Run `cargo run --release -- --sandbox --save sandbox.vps`, then press **F6**.
The catalogue opens on **Existing habitats**. Select a creature to place it
in a free spot ahead, then press **Esc** to resume. Aquatic creatures require
nearby deep water. The filter switches to **All Overworld creatures**.
**Clear nearby creatures** removes mobs within 32 blocks to make room for
more previews; it is available only through this sandbox screen.

The full catalogue also includes mobs associated with missing structures,
biomes, and creation mechanics: for example allays, armadillos, axolotls,
bogged, breezes, copper golems, creakings, guardians, happy ghasts, pandas,
sniffers, sulfur cubes, and wardens. These have models, animation, collision,
health, and basic movement/combat; their specialized mechanics are deferred.
They do not spawn naturally in unrelated habitats.

This is a custom game's roster expansion, not full Bedrock behavior parity.
Riding, taming, trading, raids, mob equipment, special attacks/status effects,
construction recipes, transformations, and species-specific item collection
are not implemented. Loot uses the game's existing items. Mobs still have the
existing session-only lifecycle: individual mobs are not stored in world saves.

The roster was checked against the official
[Bedrock entity list](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/minecraft/creator/reference/content/vanillalistingsreference/entities?view=minecraft-bedrock-stable),
[Bedrock sample version metadata](https://github.com/Mojang/bedrock-samples/blob/main/version.json),
and the released
[26.30 sulfur-cube update](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-26-30-bedrock-changelog).
Nether/End-exclusive mobs are excluded. Withers and zombified piglins are
catalogue-only because they can be created in the Overworld.

## Verification

```text
cargo test --locked --offline
cargo build --release --locked --offline
cargo run --release --locked --offline -- --smoke-test-mobs
cargo run --release --locked --offline -- --smoke-test-world
```

The creature smoke test renders every production model at rest and in motion,
captures the catalogue and a scene in Fast/Fancy graphics, and exercises water,
flight, collision, and capacity checks. It uses a hidden test window, writes
images to `target/test-artifacts/`, and leaves world saves untouched.

Dolphins use a separate body, pitching tail, child fluke, and paired pectoral
fins. Their stroke axes and cadence follow the official
[Bedrock dolphin animation reference](https://github.com/Mojang/bedrock-samples/blob/main/resource_pack/animations/dolphin.animation.json).
The creature smoke test also captures a complete 64-frame dolphin animation loop.
